Port Lincoln trophy questions

Do you deliver trophies to Port Lincoln?

Yes. Port Lincoln is about 646 km from our Adelaide showroom, and we deliver there with standard shipping at a $9.90 flat rate or express at $25, after 5 working days production.

Can you make AFL trophies for Port Lincoln clubs?

Yes. Afl and netball are among the main categories for Port Lincoln, and every item includes free engraving. Popular picks include the Sunrise Aussie Rules Medal (50mm), from $6.50. There is no minimum order.

How fast can I get trophies to Port Lincoln for a presentation day?

Standard production is 5 working days, plus delivery to Port Lincoln. Fast Track is 4 working days ($40 plus 10% of the order, capped at $300) and Priority Rush is 3 working days ($50 plus 15% of the order, capped at $300), also plus delivery. Tell us the event date when you order.

Can I collect my order instead of having it delivered to Port Lincoln?

Yes, if you are coming to Adelaide. Pickup is from 56 Prospect Road, Prospect SA 5082, Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm, with no shipping charge.
