Adelaide trophy questions

Where can I pick up trophies in Adelaide?

From our Prospect showroom at 56 Prospect Road, Prospect SA 5082, Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm. It is on Prospect Road, about 4 km north of the CBD, with plenty of on-site parking.

How fast can you engrave for a presentation night?

Standard production is 5 working days. Fast Track is 4 working days ($40 plus 10% of the order, capped at $300) and Priority Rush is 3 working days ($50 plus 15%, capped at $300). Rush pickup is $15 plus 20% of the order, capped at $300.

Do you deliver to outer suburbs like Aldinga Beach and Gawler?

Yes. Standard shipping is a $9.90 flat rate and express is $25, after 5 working days production.

Is engraving free?

Yes, free engraving on every order, and there is no minimum order.

Is there parking at the Prospect showroom?

Yes, there is plenty of on-site parking at the Prospect showroom.

Can I see products before I order?

Yes. Visit the Prospect showroom during opening hours to see samples and talk to the team.
