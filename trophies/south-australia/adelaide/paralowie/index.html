Paralowie trophy questions

Can I pick up trophies from Prospect if I live in Paralowie?

Paralowie is about 20.6 km by road from our Prospect showroom, via the Northern Expressway (M2). Google Maps shows about 24 min in afternoon traffic. Orders are ready for pickup at 56 Prospect Road, Prospect SA 5082, Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm, after 5 working days production. Priority Rush pickup is available from day 3 ($15 plus 20% of the order, capped at $300). There is plenty of on-site parking.

Do you deliver trophies to Paralowie?

Yes. Standard shipping to Paralowie is a $9.90 flat rate and express is $25, after 5 working days production. Pickup from Prospect has no shipping charge.

Can I order soccer trophies for a Paralowie club or school?

Yes. Soccer is one of the popular categories in the area, alongside AFL, basketball and netball. A good starting point is the Football Theme With Marble Base Trophy, from $8.80. Every item includes free engraving and there is no minimum order.

How fast can I get trophies for a Paralowie presentation day?

Standard production is 5 working days. Fast Track is 4 working days ($40 plus 10% of the order, capped at $300) and Priority Rush is 3 working days ($50 plus 15%, capped at $300), plus delivery or pickup. Tell us the event date when you order.
