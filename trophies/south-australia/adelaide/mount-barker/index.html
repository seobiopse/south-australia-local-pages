Mount Barker trophy questions

Can I pick up trophies from Prospect if I live in Mount Barker?

Mount Barker is about 38.3 km by road from our Prospect showroom, via the South Eastern Freeway (M1). Google Maps shows about 47 min in afternoon traffic. Orders are ready for pickup at 56 Prospect Road, Prospect SA 5082, Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm, after 5 working days production. Priority Rush pickup is available from day 3 ($15 plus 20% of the order, capped at $300). There is plenty of on-site parking.

Do you deliver trophies to Mount Barker?

Yes. Standard shipping to Mount Barker is a $9.90 flat rate and express is $25, after 5 working days production. Pickup from Prospect has no shipping charge.

Can I order AFL trophies for a Mount Barker club or school?

Yes. Afl is one of the popular categories in the area, alongside netball, cricket and equestrian. A good starting point is the Aussie Rules Wayfare Medal, from $5.00. Every item includes free engraving and there is no minimum order.

How fast can I get trophies for a Mount Barker presentation day?

Standard production is 5 working days. Fast Track is 4 working days ($40 plus 10% of the order, capped at $300) and Priority Rush is 3 working days ($50 plus 15%, capped at $300), plus delivery or pickup. Tell us the event date when you order.
