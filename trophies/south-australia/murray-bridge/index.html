Murray Bridge trophy questions

Do you deliver trophies to Murray Bridge?

Yes. Murray Bridge is about 80 km from our Adelaide showroom, and we deliver there with standard shipping at a $9.90 flat rate or express at $25, after 5 working days production.

Can you make AFL trophies for Murray Bridge clubs?

Yes. Afl and netball are among the main categories for Murray Bridge, and every item includes free engraving. Popular picks include the Global Medal-Aussie Rules, from $6.50. There is no minimum order.

How fast can I get trophies to Murray Bridge for a presentation day?

Standard production is 5 working days, plus delivery to Murray Bridge. Fast Track is 4 working days ($40 plus 10% of the order, capped at $300) and Priority Rush is 3 working days ($50 plus 15% of the order, capped at $300), also plus delivery. Tell us the event date when you order.

Can I collect my order instead of having it delivered to Murray Bridge?

Yes, if you are coming to Adelaide. Pickup is from 56 Prospect Road, Prospect SA 5082, Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm, with no shipping charge.
